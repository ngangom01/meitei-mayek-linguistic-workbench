# Lean delivery plan

## Step 1 — Connect the real Academy data (first)
- Inspect the current Academy character, combination, lesson, and audio schemas.
- Confirm which records the expert should review.
- Implement the small adapter and import the assigned records.
- Do not change the Academy repository's schema just to fit this Workbench.

## Step 2 — Expert review and local save
- Show the assigned records.
- Let the expert edit only approved review fields.
- Save locally and recover after a browser restart.

## Step 3 — Pronunciation recording
- Record, play, retake, and keep multiple samples where required.
- Store original audio and metadata.

## Step 4 — Backup and submission
- Export/import a complete workspace backup.
- Export a review ZIP in the Academy-compatible format.
- Share through the native share sheet or download.

## Step 5 — Real-device test
Test at least:
- Android Chrome
- iPhone Safari
- Windows Chrome or Edge
- One tablet if available

Pilot with the expert before adding anything else.

## Working estimate

Approximately 8–12 focused development days after the Academy schema and assigned content are available. This is a practical estimate, not a commitment; real-device audio and sharing tests may expose browser-specific work.
